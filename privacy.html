Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

LiftLog is a workout log. This page explains what it stores about you, who can see it, and how to remove it.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, your name, and a securely hashed password.
  • Your training: your plan, the exercises, weights, reps and dates you log, set changes, and the optional profile you enter (body weight, age, height) used for calorie estimates.
  • Gym partners: the names and numbers you enter for people you lift with. Only you can see them. Please only add people who are fine with it.
  • Programs you publish: the program name, description, exercises and your name, plus likes and "using" counts.

Who can see it

  • Your logs, plan, profile and partner numbers are private to your account. Database rules stop other users from reading them.
  • Programs you publish, with your name, are visible to everyone signed in to LiftLog until you unpublish them.
  • We don't sell your data, and LiftLog has no ads or third-party tracking.

Usage stats

To improve LiftLog we look at totals, never at any one person's workouts: for example how many people signed up or logged a workout this week, which exercises are most popular, and how many people follow each shared program.

If you publish a program, you'll see counts for it: how many people started it, follow it, liked it, and how many workouts its followers logged. You won't see who they are or what they lifted.

Where it's kept

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in, United States) and the app is served by Vercel. Fonts load from Google Fonts. These providers process data only to run the service.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Log tab → Account → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Log tab → Account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, logs and published programs.
  • Questions or requests: zedannguyen07@gmail.com.

Children

LiftLog is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will be announced in the app.
